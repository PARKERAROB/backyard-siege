Backyard Siege

Toby versus everything with fur or feathers.
The squirrels are organizing. Mockingbirds, possums, raccoons, and moles are with them, and they all want the porch. Toby is on duty: tap anywhere on the yard to send him, and he bites whatever he reaches. Build defenses on the grass, not on the path, water, or rocks. Every map you survive unlocks the next, and fewer lives lost means more stars.
